Thomson Reserve questions, answered

Short answers from the latest availability, updated 15 Nov 2026.

How many units are left at Thomson Reserve?

As of 15 Nov 2026, 119 of the 1,268 homes at Thomson Reserve are still available: 29 2-Bedroom (592 sq ft), 89 2-Bedroom Premium (678 sq ft) and 1 5-Bedroom Suite (1,808 sq ft). They are spread over 15 stacks.

What are the prices of the remaining Thomson Reserve units?

Indicative prices as of 15 Nov 2026: 2-Bedroom from $1.643M to $1.723M ($2,775 to $2,910 psf); 2-Bedroom Premium from $1.819M to $2.217M ($2,683 to $3,270 psf); the 5-Bedroom Suite at $5.079M ($2,809 psf). Prices change without notice and the developer's price list sets each unit's price.

Are there 3-bedroom or 4-bedroom units left at Thomson Reserve?

No 3-bedroom or 4-bedroom units show as available as of 15 Nov 2026. What remains is 2-Bedroom, 2-Bedroom Premium and one 5-Bedroom Suite.

Which Thomson Reserve stacks still have 2-bedroom units?

2-Bedroom (592 sq ft): Stack 07 (Blk 1, faces north-north-west, 3 left); Stack 08 (Blk 1, faces north-north-west, 7 left); Stack 16 (Blk 3, faces north-north-east, 6 left); Stack 17 (Blk 3, faces north-north-east, 5 left); Stack 52 (Blk 11, faces south-west, 8 left).

2-Bedroom Premium (678 sq ft): Stack 20 (Blk 5, faces south-west, 9 left); Stack 21 (Blk 5, faces south-west, 4 left); Stack 26 (Blk 5, faces north-east, 23 left); Stack 29 (Blk 7, faces north-west, 24 left); Stack 30 (Blk 7, faces north-west, 13 left); Stack 35 (Blk 7, faces south-east, 11 left); Stack 38 (Blk 9, faces north-north-east, 3 left); Stack 42 (Blk 9, faces south-south-west, 1 left); Stack 53 (Blk 11, faces south-west, 1 left).

How much more does a higher floor cost at Thomson Reserve?

Among the units still available, each storey up typically adds about $6,000 to $8,000 for a 2-Bedroom and about $7,000 to $9,000 for a 2-Bedroom Premium within the same stack. The step is not uniform: some floors jump by more. This is worked out from indicative asking prices as of 15 Nov 2026.

Which Thomson Reserve stacks get morning sun and which get afternoon sun?

By balcony facing, of the stacks with homes left:

  • Morning sun side: Stack 26, Stack 35
  • Afternoon sun side: Stack 19, Stack 20, Stack 21, Stack 29, Stack 30, Stack 52, Stack 53
  • Roughly north-facing: Stack 07, Stack 08, Stack 16, Stack 17, Stack 38
  • Roughly south-facing: Stack 42

These are modelled from the developer's plans and may differ on site. The 3D model shows where sun lands inside each unit, by time, day and year.

What is the difference between the Classic and Luxury Collection blocks?

The Classic Collection is Blocks 1, 3, 9 and 11, each 21 storeys. The Luxury Collection is Blocks 5 and 7, each 30 storeys. Each stack page here states which collection it belongs to.

Where is Thomson Reserve and which MRT stations are nearest?

Thomson Reserve is at 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 and 11 Bright Hill Drive in District 20, Singapore. The nearest MRT stations are Upper Thomson (TE8) and Bright Hill (TE7) on the Thomson-East Coast Line. It is a 99-year leasehold development of 1,268 homes in 6 blocks by CapitaLand Development, UOL Group and Singapore Land Group.
